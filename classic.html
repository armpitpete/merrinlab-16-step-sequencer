MerrinLab Classic

Classic 16-Step Sequencer

Original-style placeholder only. The full Classic UI has not been built yet.

Status

Classic / Original is the faithful Ray-style 16-step quantized sequencer panel.

Step rows

Active Step, Gate At Step Selection, Coarse Voltage, Fine Voltage, and Step Duration rows are required.

Duration matrix

The Step Duration LED matrix is required. Do not replace it with only a simple 4-LED display.

Clock

Master Clock Rate, Clock Source, Clock Out, and External Clock In belong to the Classic panel.

Sequencer mode

Sequencer Mode, Action At Step, Action Step, and manual control sections are part of the original layout.

Outputs

Trigger Out, Gate Out, Multi-Gate / Trigger, Control Voltage Out, and Portamento are required.

Not digital

No Bank A/B, no 32-step mode, no Play Range, and no M/S/A/G Clean controls on the Classic panel.

Step Duration LED Matrix Required

The Classic panel must include the original Step Duration LED matrix section with 8 / 4 / 2 / 1 duration labelling. This is part of the Classic identity and must not be simplified away.
